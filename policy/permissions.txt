# The permissions the release APK requests, one per line, exactly (MILESTONE_51_ANDROID_CI_CD.md
# section 3, the policy job; scripts/check_release_policy.sh). A permission is added here in the same
# change that needs it, with the design section that asks for it.

# core-transport's network callbacks, behind the reachability facts of design section 5.2.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
# The scan's camera, onboarding's step 3 (design sections 12.1 and 13.3), asked for after its rationale,
# and the attach sheet's Camera (design section 8.5); no camera is a required feature, as the pasted
# link is the other way in.
android.permission.CAMERA
# The short foreground services of design section 12.5: the pairing wait's and an upload's.
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE
# The WebSocket to a daemon (design section 5.1), core-transport's.
android.permission.INTERNET
# Onboarding's step 7 asks for it (design section 13.3), and the pairing wait posts with it.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS
# The voice note, asked for on the first hold of the mic after its rationale (design section 8.5).
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO
# The app lock's prompt, BIOMETRIC_STRONG | DEVICE_CREDENTIAL (design section 13.7).
android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC
# Firebase Messaging's (design section 10): it holds the phone awake while a push is taken to its
# notification, which onMessageReceived does before it returns.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK
# FCM's delivery of a push to this app (design section 10), declared by Firebase Messaging, whose
# receiver takes it from Google Play services alone (com.google.android.c2dm.permission.SEND).
com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE
